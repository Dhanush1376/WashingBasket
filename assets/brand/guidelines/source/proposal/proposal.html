Executive overview

A connected service platform, not a brochure app

Washing Basket is being developed as a connected digital service platform for real customers and real payments: Android and iOS apps, backend services, an operations portal, a live payment gateway, testing, store release and a codebase the client owns.

The goal is to make the customer journey straightforward while giving the business a dependable foundation to manage bookings, payments, service progress, customer information and future growth. The platform should reduce manual coordination, prevent avoidable operational errors and keep the service experience consistent.

Proposed scope baseline

01Customer app

React Native + Expo for Android and iOS: phone-number/OTP sign-in, profile, service discovery, booking and order views.

02Service journey

Collect → Wash → Dry → Iron → Handover, with clear, understandable status updates at every step.

03Live payments and wallet

Wallet top-up and checkout through a live payment gateway, configured bonus logic, coupons at the payment stage, and QR access after wallet payment.

04Order operations

Backend order lifecycle with customer-facing status visibility, built for real customers from day one.

05Admin interface

Web portal for the agreed customer, booking/order, payment and status-management tasks, run by the client’s own team.

06Technical foundation

Node.js/Express APIs, MongoDB, validation, authentication, access controls, security hardening, testing and production deployment.

Two phases

Phase 1: clickable prototype. The full flow with sample data, so Washing Basket can see the experience before committing. Phase 2: full build. On approval, the prototype becomes the real product with live payments and real users.

Why this is more than app design

Two platforms

Android and iOS need their own implementation, device testing, release configuration and platform-specific checks.

Real money, real users

Payments, refunds and settlements must be correct every time, across the app, backend, gateway and admin portal.

Data and security

Customer records, authentication, permissions, validation, backups and controlled access need deliberate engineering.

03
Client inputs, ownership and warranty

What we need, and what you own

Delivery dates depend on the following inputs being available when each stage needs them.

  • One authorised point of contact for consolidated feedback and approvals.
  • Final service catalogue, service areas, pricing, taxes/fees and booking constraints.
  • Confirmed order lifecycle, cancellation/refund rules, wallet bonus, coupon conditions and QR unlock criteria.
  • Approved brand assets, images, content, privacy policy and legal text.
  • Business accounts in Washing Basket’s name: Google Play, Apple Developer, payment gateway (KYC), SMS/DLT and domain.
  • Timely feedback, ideally within 2–3 working days for each review milestone.

60-day warranty

Every reproducible defect in the delivered scope is fixed free of charge for 60 days after go-live. Warranty fixes never use maintenance plan hours.

It excludes new requirements, third-party outages, client configuration changes and issues caused by unauthorised code changes.

You own the product

The source code lives in a GitHub account owned by Washing Basket, with full code and IP ownership transferring on final payment.

Handover includes two admin training sessions and an admin user guide for the team that will run the portal.
